Rangbhoomi — a map of Indian art history

Sixteen places where a distinct Indian art movement took root, plotted across the subcontinent and across roughly 30,000 years — from the ochre hand of Bhimbetka to the artist-run village of Cholamandal. Click a place, on the map or in the index, for its images, artists, and historical context.

How to use this map

  • Use the era chips above to isolate one artistic period, or leave them all on to see the full spread.
  • Click any dot on the map, or any row in the index below, to open that site’s history.
  • Marker colour encodes era — matched to the chips and the index groups.
